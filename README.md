# Healthcare Analytics & Operations Dashboard

## Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Modeling | Data Visualization

---

## 1. Project Overview

The **Healthcare Analytics & Operations Dashboard** is an end-to-end data analytics project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to transform raw healthcare data into an interactive analytical dashboard.

The project focuses on understanding healthcare operations from multiple perspectives, including:

- Patient activity
- Hospital operations
- Department performance
- Admissions and visits
- Treatment patterns
- Waiting time
- Length of stay
- Resource utilization
- Treatment costs
- Revenue and billing
- Patient and operational trends

The project follows a complete analytics workflow starting from **business problem definition and data collection** and continuing through **data validation, cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX analysis, dashboard development, validation, and insight generation**.

The objective is not simply to create charts, but to build a structured analytical solution where the underlying data is properly prepared, modeled, analyzed, and converted into meaningful business insights.

---

# 2. Project Objectives

The primary objective of the project is to develop an interactive healthcare analytics solution that can help stakeholders understand operational and financial performance.

The project aims to:

1. Collect and organize relevant healthcare datasets.
2. Understand the structure and quality of the raw data.
3. Identify data-quality issues before analysis.
4. Clean and standardize the datasets.
5. Transform raw fields into useful analytical attributes.
6. Design a structured relational data model.
7. Build a dedicated Date dimension for time-based analysis.
8. Establish appropriate relationships between fact and dimension tables.
9. Develop reusable DAX measures and KPIs.
10. Build an interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard.
11. Validate dashboard calculations against the source data.
12. Identify trends, patterns, operational bottlenecks, and performance variations.
13. Convert analytical findings into meaningful business insights.

---

# 3. Business Problem

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of operational and transactional data across different areas such as patients, visits, admissions, treatments, departments, doctors, hospitals, and billing.

When this information exists across separate datasets, it becomes difficult to obtain a unified view of healthcare performance.

The dashboard addresses this problem by integrating relevant datasets into a structured analytical model and providing a centralized interface for exploring healthcare operations.

### Key business questions addressed

The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

### Patient Analytics

- How many patients are being served?
- How is patient activity changing over time?
- What are the major patient demographic groups?
- Which age groups contribute the highest patient volume?
- How does patient activity vary across hospitals and departments?

### Hospital Operations

- How many visits and admissions are recorded?
- Which hospitals have the highest patient activity?
- How does operational activity change over time?
- What is the average length of stay?
- Which areas experience higher waiting times?

### Department Performance

- Which departments handle the highest number of visits?
- Which departments have higher waiting times?
- How does treatment activity vary by department?
- How does departmental performance change over time?

### Treatment Analysis

- Which treatments are most frequently performed?
- What are the associated treatment costs?
- How do treatment patterns vary between departments?
- Which treatment categories contribute most to healthcare activity?

### Financial Analysis

- What is the total recorded revenue?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which departments generate higher revenue?
- How do treatment costs vary across categories?
- What is the distribution of billing and payment information?

### Operational Insights

- Where are potential operational bottlenecks?
- Which departments show higher workload?
- Which areas require closer monitoring?
- What trends can support data-driven operational decisions?
